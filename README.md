Solmar City is a sun-soaked open-world crime game built with three.js: explore a coastal city of five districts on foot and by car, steal any vehicle, outrun the police across five wanted levels, and work off a debt across 8 story missions for fixer Iris Delgado. Traffic, pedestrians, shootouts, taxi fares, hidden packages, a save system and procedural audio — all original, all offline.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080.

## Controls

- **On foot:** WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, mouse to look, E enter a car, F punch, click to shoot, R reload, 1–4 switch weapons, P pause, M mute.
- **Driving:** W/S throttle and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake, Shift boost in chases, E exit the car.
- **Heat:** stealing cars, hitting people and shooting raise your wanted stars. Hide out of sight to cool off, or pay $150 at the Tune-Up Garage. Getting busted or wasted sends you to the hospital — and costs cash.
- **Money:** missions, taxi fares and packages ($50 each, $500 for all 20). Spend it at Harbor Hardware (weapons) and Marina Motors (cars).

## The city

Five districts — Marina Bay, Old Town, Harbor Docks, Downtown and North Hills — plus a beach, Pier 9, the Old Lighthouse and Estadio del Sol. Six invented car models (Alba, Meridian, Vela GT, Hauler, Dune Runner, Solmar Taxi), a police Interceptor, and a helicopter spotlight at 3+ stars. Every name, place, car and character is invented for this game.
