SOLMAR CITY

Sun-soaked streets, fast cars, and a debt to pay. Juno Reyes owes fixer Iris Delgado — work it off across 8 story missions, steal what you need, and stay ahead of the Solmar police.

On foot: WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump, mouse to look, E enter a car, F punch, click to shoot, R reload, 1–4 switch weapons.
Driving: W/S throttle and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake, Shift boost in chases, E exit.
Heat: stealing cars, hitting people and shooting raise your wanted stars. Hide out of sight to cool off, or pay $150 at the Tune-Up Garage. Busted or wasted means the hospital — and lost cash.
Money: missions, taxi fares, packages ($50 each, $500 for all 20). Spend it at Harbor Hardware (weapons) and Marina Motors (cars).
